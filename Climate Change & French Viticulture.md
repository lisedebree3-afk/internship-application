Group academic research project

Economics / Environmental & Agricultural Economics

Overview

This project examines the relationship between climate change and wine production in two major French wine regions: Bourgogne-Franche-Comté and Nouvelle-Aquitaine.

The study investigates how temperature, precipitation, drought and heatwaves may affect wine yields and wine quality. It combines climate and viticultural production data and examines both annual and seasonal relationships.

Le projet couvre la période 1994–2023 dans les deux régions et utilise notamment des données de FranceAgriMer et de Météo-France.

Research question

How do climate variables affect wine yield and quality in Bourgogne-Franche-Comté and Nouvelle-Aquitaine?

Methodology

Geographical scope: Bourgogne-Franche-Comté & Nouvelle-Aquitaine

Period analysed: 1994–2023

Data: FranceAgriMer & Météo-France

Variables: Temperature · Precipitation · Drought · Heatwaves · Wine production

Method: Annual and seasonal regression analysis

Key outcomes

The analysis examines the relationship between climate conditions and wine production.

Seasonal climate conditions are considered alongside annual conditions.

The project explores whether different wine classification categories respond differently to climate conditions.
